A CLUB FOR EVERY KIND OF READER

Good books. Better company.

Welcome to The Occasional Bookworms Reading Club: a gathering place for curious readers, book lovers, and anyone looking to discover the joy of reading. There's no need to commit to a particular book, reading schedule, or goal. Everyone has their own pace, preferences, and reasons for reading, and that's part of what makes this a unique reading club. Come enjoy a little coffee, some pastries, good conversation, and the occasional book discussion.
